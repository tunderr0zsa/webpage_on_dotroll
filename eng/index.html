A little story behind my creations

🎨 I used to paint mostly canvas pictures, and over time I made so many that it became increasingly difficult to find space for them. Then, in Portugal, I came across hand-painted clothes. I loved the artist’s work so much that I bought a denim jacket and a sweater right there. I liked not only the clothes, but also the fact that my purchase supported an artist.

👚 That’s when I had the idea: why not paint on clothes myself?

✨ I wanted to make things that wouldn’t just hang on a wall or sit in the corner of a room, but could become part of everyday life. So I started painting clothes first for myself, and then for friends and family.

♻️ I’ve really fallen in love with this kind of creative work. I still love painting, of course, but one thing I especially enjoy about it is giving new life to clothes that might otherwise have become forgotten. With a little paint and creativity, they can become someone else’s new favorite.

❣️ That’s why, to me, hand-painted clothes are not just one-of-a-kind pieces; they’re also a little about sustainability: fewer new things, more new life.
